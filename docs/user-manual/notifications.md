# Notifications

Knowcelium tells you when something you started has finished, for example an upload that was processed. A short message appears for a few seconds, and the notifications panel keeps the most recent ones, so you can see what finished while you were away.

## Open the notifications panel

Select the bell at the top of the sidebar. On a phone it is at the top of the page, next to the menu button. A number on the bell shows how many notifications you haven't read yet.

The panel lists your notifications with the newest first. Unread ones are in bold with a dot. Each shows what happened and how long ago. Select **Esc**, the bell or anywhere outside the panel to close it.

When there are none, the panel says "Finished uploads and answers show up here."

## Open what a notification is about

Select a notification. Knowcelium opens the page it is about, for example the document that was uploaded, and marks the notification as read. A notification without a page is only marked as read.

## Mark notifications as read

Select **Mark all as read** at the top of the panel. The number on the bell goes away.

## Clear notifications

1. Open the notifications panel.
2. Select **Clear all**.
3. Confirm with **Clear all** in the dialog, or select **Cancel** to keep them.

Clearing removes every notification, read or not.

## How long they are kept

Knowcelium keeps your 50 most recent notifications in this browser, so they are still there after you reload the page or come back later. Older ones make room for new ones. They belong to your account: someone else who signs in on the same browser doesn't see them, and you don't see them in another browser.
